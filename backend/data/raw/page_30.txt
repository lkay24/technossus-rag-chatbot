Enabling Real-Time Suspect Identification with Computer Vision
CLIENT
A leading UK law enforcement agency sought to enhance frontline policing capabilities through secure mobile access to real-time facial recognition technology.
CHALLENGES
Limited Access to AI-Powered Intelligence:
The existing facial recognition platform was designed for office-based users, preventing field officers from leveraging AI-powered suspect identification capabilities during active investigations.
Delayed Identification: Without real-time access to facial recognition systems, officers relied on manual processes and centralized support teams, slowing response times.
Operational Inefficiencies: Critical intelligence could not be accessed at the point of need, impacting field productivity and situational awareness.
Security & Compliance Requirements:
The organization required a secure mobile solution that could extend AI capabilities beyond traditional desktop environments while maintaining strict security controls.
SOLUTIONS
Technossus developed an AI-enabled mobile identification platform that brought facial recognition capabilities directly to officers in the field.
The solution included:
Mobile AI Recognition: Enabled officers to capture and submit facial images through a secure mobile application for real-time identification.
Facial Recognition Integration: Connected the application to an enterprise facial recognition platform capable of matching images against known suspect databases.
Intelligent Search & Matching: Leveraged AI-driven biometric matching algorithms to rapidly identify individuals and return potential matches.
Secure Operational Platform: Implemented secure middleware, encrypted communications, and centralized monitoring to ensure compliance and operational integrity.
