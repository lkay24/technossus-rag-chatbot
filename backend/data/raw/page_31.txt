Leveraging AI-driven document processing to streamline public safety operations
CLIENT
A leading public safety organization in the United Kingdom responsible for managing traffic enforcement operations and processing thousands of Traffic Offense Reports annually. The organization required a scalable and intelligent solution to streamline case creation, improve processing accuracy, and reduce administrative workloads.
CHALLENGES
Manual Processing Bottlenecks:
Each report required manual review, validation, and entry into downstream systems, creating delays.
High Administrative Effort:
Case creation consumed significant staff time and resources.
Error-Prone Operations:
Manual data entry increased the risk of invalid reports and rework.
Growing Backlogs:
The volume of incoming reports led to large queues of unprocessed cases.
SOLUTIONS
Technossus developed an intelligent automation solution leveraging AI-powered OCR and workflow automation.
The solution included:
Intelligent Document Processing:
Automatically extracted data from Traffic Offense Reports received via email.
Automated Validation:
Validated report information against government databases and predefined business rules.
End-to-End Workflow Automation:
Automatically created cases and generated penalties without human intervention.
Event-Driven Processing:
Triggered workflows instantly upon report receipt, enabling real-time processing.
